Reflection & Where I’m Headed

I started in sales at Best Buy as a Computing Sales Advisor. Being in person-to-person sales taught me the foundation of sales, but it ignited a desire to help more people at once. I landed in tech sales, where I initially focused too heavily on product knowledge. I finally understood that the real value in a deal was the trust between the client and the advisor. The deal that taught me this lesson was one with a daycare, where I prioritized the needs of the children before diving into the features of our software. That turned into one of the biggest sales I’ve ever closed.
